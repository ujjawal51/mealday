# Essential setup

New kitchens complete four screens: (1) household size, cooking days and meal types; (2) diet and an explicit ingredient-exclusion choice; (3) grocery budget and maximum cooking time; (4) available appliances and a final summary.

Name is optional. New kitchens default to Any supermarket, Dubai and UAE mix, displayed in the review. Names, city, supermarket, cuisines and meal styles are edited under Preferences → Personalise further. Diet, exclusions, appliances, meals, days, servings, budget and time are directly editable in Preferences. Settings → Profile & food preferences opens this editor rather than replaying setup.

Existing saved profiles and plans are retained. Setup Back preserves draft selections. Progress shows 1–4. No day/meal, unconfirmed exclusions, invalid budgets and no selected appliance block progression. Plan generation remains on-device.
